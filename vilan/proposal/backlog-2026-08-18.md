5. **`VILAN_PHASE_TIMING` cannot see inside `post_analysis_passes`** (S; found by M1)
   STATUS: OPEN

6. **The in-process cold measurement cannot clear the parse cache** (S; found by M1)
   STATUS: OPEN
   `analyzer::base_cache_clear()` and `macro_world_cache_clear()` are
   both `#[doc(hidden)] pub`; `parse_clean_cached`'s content-keyed store
   of module texts and ASTs (lib.rs:218) has no clearer, so the
   harness's "cold" is *world-cold, parse-warm* and its first-measured

   (perf-baseline.md §1 — the harness primes every subject once to
   cancel the ordering artifact, which costs a compile per subject and
   still leaves the true first-compile shape unmeasurable in-process).

   siblings would close it; the memory stays leaked either way, so the
   clearer only drops the map. Weigh against: the end-to-end section

8. **NEW — the const pass's evaluator leaves `Rc` cycles every analysis: +1.52 MiB on page.vl** (M; found by M7's lane 2026-08-19 — the remainder under M7)
   STATUS: OPEN
   With M7's entry reclaim in, `mallinfo2` still shows in-use bytes growing
   **+1,523.9 KiB per analysis on `vilan-website/src/page.vl`** and +45.8
   KiB on `kolt/src/views.vl`, flat to the KiB across windows, `fordblks`
   ~0 — a genuine leak, not fragmentation. Mechanism (`interpreter.rs`):
   `Scope` is `Rc<RefCell<..>>`, hoisted functions are

   35 sites; views.vl reaches std's const-evaluated style validation).

   nested function-in-function cycles need a per-run scope registry

   equivalence gate. This is the item that makes "bounded in the session"
